Our approach

Questions people ask

Who is Hon. Engr. Akarachi Amadi?

He is the Member representing Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency in the House of Representatives, a chemical engineer and a businessman from Eziama, Ikeduru.

How do I apply?

Create an account with your phone number, choose a programme and complete the form. You can leave at any point and sign back in to continue where you stopped.

Who is eligible?

Each programme lists its own conditions. All three are for indigenes of Mbaitoli/Ikeduru; the growth programme also accepts residents with a CAC-registered business.

How do I check my application?

Sign in and open your dashboard, or enter your reference number on the Check status page. You will see the stage your application has reached.

Does it cost anything, or depend on who I support?

No. Applying is free. Selection follows the published criteria and is never tied to party membership or endorsement.

Your application takes minutes to start.
