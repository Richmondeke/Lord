ONE-PAGE BUSINESS PLAN
Empowerment Portal, AAK Legacy Foundation - used in week 3 of the learning track

Keep each answer to two or three sentences. Plain words are best.

1. YOUR BUSINESS IN ONE SENTENCE
   What do you sell or make, and where?

2. YOUR CUSTOMERS
   Who buys from you? How many do you serve in a normal week?

3. THE PROBLEM YOU SOLVE
   Why do people need what you offer? What do they do today without you?

4. WHAT YOU NEED
   The equipment or support you are asking for, and its approximate cost.

5. HOW IT WILL GROW THE BUSINESS
   What will change once you have it? (More customers, more output, less cost.)

6. MONEY IN AND MONEY OUT
   Expected sales each month:        N________
   Expected costs each month:        N________
   Expected profit each month:       N________
   (Use the cash book to track these once you start.)

7. RISKS AND HOW YOU WILL MANAGE THEM
   What could go wrong (breakdown, low season, theft), and what will you do?

8. YOUR GOALS FOR THE NEXT 6 MONTHS
   Two or three things you will have achieved, with numbers where you can.
