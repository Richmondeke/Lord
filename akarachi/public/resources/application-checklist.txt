EMPOWERMENT PORTAL - APPLICATION CHECKLIST
AAK Legacy Foundation, Mbaitoli/Ikeduru

Applying is free. Nobody should ask you to pay at any stage.
Questions: info@aaklegacy.org

Gather everything for your programme before you start. Clear photos or scans
are fine; make sure every word can be read.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. EDUCATION EMPOWERMENT
------------------------------------------------------------
You qualify if:
[ ] You are an indigene of Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency
[ ] You scored 200 or above in the UTME (JAMB)
[ ] You have admission to a Federal or State tertiary institution
[ ] You have not received this education support before

Have these ready:
[ ] JAMB result slip
[ ] Admission letter
[ ] School fee invoice or evidence of registration
[ ] Valid means of identification
[ ] Recent passport photograph
[ ] Certificate of origin

------------------------------------------------------------
2. NEW BUSINESS START-UP SUPPORT
------------------------------------------------------------
You qualify if:
[ ] You are an indigene of Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency
[ ] You are 18 or older
[ ] You work in, or are ready to start, a productive trade (farming, petty
    trading, food processing, transport, artisanship, fishing)
[ ] Your Traditional Ruler will attest to your application
[ ] You submit one application only, and can be visited for verification

Have these ready:
[ ] Valid means of identification
[ ] Attestation from your Traditional Ruler (see the attestation template)
[ ] Recent passport photograph
[ ] Business plan or one-page summary (see the one-page business plan)

------------------------------------------------------------
3. EXISTING BUSINESS GROWTH SUPPORT
------------------------------------------------------------
You qualify if:
[ ] You are an indigene or resident of Mbaitoli/Ikeduru Federal Constituency
[ ] You are 18 or older
[ ] Your business is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
[ ] Your Traditional Ruler will attest to your application
[ ] You submit one application only, and can be visited for verification

Have these ready:
[ ] Valid means of identification
[ ] CAC registration certificate
[ ] Attestation from your Traditional Ruler
[ ] Photograph of your shop, farm or workshop

------------------------------------------------------------
AFTER YOU SUBMIT
------------------------------------------------------------
Your application moves through five stages: Submitted, Under review,
Community verification, Approved, Delivered. Follow it from your dashboard, or enter your
reference number on the Check status page.
