Design system

The rules both sites are built from. The main site borrows its structure from obama.org: photographs at full width, condensed headlines, a heavy rule to open each section and flat blocks of colour with square corners.

Type

obama.org sets headlines in Gotham Condensed and text in Chronicle Text. Both are commercial typefaces from Hoefler&Co and cannot be used without a licence, so this build uses the nearest open equivalents. If the office buys the licences, the swap is three lines in the root layout.

Motion

Page change
The old page settles back and dims; the new one wipes up over it. 720ms. The header holds still.
Hero
Black and white only. The headline rises from a mask; the portrait settles onto the floor of the hero.
Headlines
Words rise out of a mask one after another, once, when first scrolled into view.
Photographs
Wipe open from the bottom edge while settling from a slight zoom.
Numbers
Count up once.
Portraits
Drift a few pixels against the scroll.
Reduced motion
All of the above switch off; content appears in place.
